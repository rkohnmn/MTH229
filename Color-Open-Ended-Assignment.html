gf_raster(Z~X+Y, data = df.volcano)  %>% gf_refine( scale_fill_viridis(direction = -1, option = "magma" ))%>%gf_theme(theme_void()) %>% gf_labs(x = "", y = "", title="Volcano Topography  Plot" , subtitle="Raster Plot of Volcano Elevation", caption = "df.volcano",fill = "Elevation(m)")%>% gf_theme(plot.title = element_text(hjust = 0.5, face = "bold"),axis.title.y = element_text(angle = 0, vjust = 0.5), legend.title = element_text(size = 10))%>%gf_refine(coord_fixed(ratio = 1))

The Volcano Plot was kind of bland in the original plot. I felt like I needed to touch it up more in this. For starters, I prefer associating higher elevations with darker colors while the lower values are ‘empty’ so to speak and thefore closer to white. I liked the color scheme in magma though so I just reversed the direction. I also added topography to the title as I believed it would help better describe what we are looking at. In the subtitle I decided to include plot type, being raster. Additionally, I felt like x and y complicated things such that removing them was optimal. Additionally, I relabed the key to be Elevation in terms of meters. then applied a fixed ratio.
