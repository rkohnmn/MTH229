Election08$Winner <- ifelse(Election08$ObamaWin == 1, "Obama", "McCain")

print("The Following is my Original Plot from Begin Graphing in RStudio Open Ended")
## [1] "The Following is my Original Plot from Begin Graphing in RStudio Open Ended"
gf_violin( Winner ~ Income , data = Election08 ,title="Violin Plot of Per Capita Income vs Winner of State")

print("The Following is my Revised Plot from Begin Graphing in RStudio Open Ended")
## [1] "The Following is my Revised Plot from Begin Graphing in RStudio Open Ended"
gf_violin( Winner ~ Income , data = Election08, fill = ~Winner)%>%gf_labs(x = "The Per Capita Income of the State", y = "Candidate", title="Violin Plot of Per Capita Income vs Winner of State" , subtitle = "The Results from the 2008 United States Election" , caption = "Dataset: Election08(Stat2Data)")

I feel like the difference between the two is night and day fully. Not only are the individual violins filled in with the proper party colors but this whole graph looks relatively professional. It shows the candidates along side the per capita income and clearly labeled key and title. I am very content with this one.
